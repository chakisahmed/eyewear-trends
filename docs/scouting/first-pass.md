## Reading it
- **Four Shopify brands look like Etnia-class work** (Cubitts, Dita, Barton Perreira, E.B. Meyrowitz): frames and colour swatches are served in the HTML, paging is a `rel=next` link, and three of them carry JSON-LD prices. Cubitts and Dita are the best first candidates.
- **Two are presence-only, like Morel** (J.F. Rey, Kuboraum): frames are listed but no price is shown.
- **Silhouette** is behind a bot challenge (client's help needed); **Rigards** publishes no frames at all and **Alain Mikli** is a showcase.
- **Locale matters:** Dita sent us to its Tunisian store. Pick the market to track for each brand, ideally Europe or the US.
- The first pass only reads what a plain HTTP client is served. It cannot judge terms of use, so `notes.md` still asks for that.
