# Eyewear brands known online for acetate creativity

Brands whose acetate work (colour, translucency, tortoise and marble patterns, layering, thick sculpted shapes) is what
they are known for online. This is a candidate list for the radar, not a client decision, and nothing here is crawled
unless the last column says so.

**How it was made:** from my own knowledge of the sector, **not** from a web check. Domains and descriptions were not
verified in this pass. Before scouting a brand, run the plain HTTP check described in `docs/reference-brands.md`.

**Scope rule:** the cadrage (section 2.1) excludes **Asia**. Asian brands are not in the main tables; they are set aside
at the end with the reason. Priority markets are Europe, USA, Maghreb, Tunisia.

## Notes
- **Acetate suppliers are not brands.** Much of the creativity above comes from sheet makers such as Mazzucchelli (Italy),
  which supply the layered, marbled and translucent sheets. A supplier is a source for material trends, not a frame brand.
- **Bio-acetates:** the cadrage (section 2.2) lists them as a material to watch. Which of these brands offer one was not
  checked here.
- **Likely next picks** (design-led, small catalogs, close to what is already crawled): Cutler and Gross, Kirk & Kirk,
  Retrosuperfuture, Moscot, Anne et Valentin.
